**`radar watch` shows a small card in the tab you're on when an agent in another session gets blocked, fails or finishes.**

## Why

Rex sends macOS notifications only while it's in the background. While you work in Rex, an agent in another session could wait unseen.

## How it works

The card appears in the top right of your tab without taking focus. Click it to jump to the agent. It closes after 30 seconds, or as soon as the agent moves on. `--states blocked,error` leaves out finished agents.

`radar watch --plist` prints a LaunchAgent so it runs at login. It waits for Rex instead of starting it, and only one runs at a time. See [Toasts from other sessions](/docs/quick-start#toasts-from-other-sessions).

## Limits

Rex doesn't publish which session you're looking at, so the watcher reads it from the Rex app's preferences. A Rex update could move it. If that happens, the log at `~/Library/Logs/radar-watch.log` says why toasts stopped.
